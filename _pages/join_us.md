## For prospective HDR (PhD/MPhil/MRes) students:
---
### 1) How to Apply

#### Quick path (recommended)
1.  **Select a project/topic** in Robotics and/or AI related to ERA Lab's focuses. You may also explore other projects and supervisors at the page [Higher Degree by Research](https://research.curtin.edu.au/higher-degree-by-research/).
2.  **Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI)** — mention your proposed topic, preferred supervisor(s), CV, transcripts: [EOI form (Curtin Forms)](https://forms.curtin.edu.au/Produce/wizard/791e8f99-aacb-41ad-9f83-b2cceebab565/).
3.  **Draft a research proposal** (topic, plan, objectives, significance, ethics): guidance under [How to apply—proposal](https://www.curtin.edu.au/study/applying/research/).
4.  **Apply** formally once your supervisor interest is confirmed: follow “Apply now” from the HDR course page: [How to apply for a research degree](https://www.curtin.edu.au/study/applying/research/).
> **Master of Research (MRes)**: a one‑year extension of an **end‑on Honours** research project; see the MRes info sheet (eligibility, RTP fee offsets, scholarships). [MRes information sheet (PDF)](https://scholarships.curtin.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/2021/10/MRes-Information-Sheet-20211025-1.pdf).

#### Entry & English requirements
*   Curtin lists accepted English tests and qualifying pathways (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL, etc.). Check your course’s entry tab + the general pages: [English requirements](https://www.curtin.edu.au/study/applying/english-language-requirements/) and [Accepted English qualifications](https://www.curtin.edu.au/study/applying/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-qualifications/).

#### International applicants

*   Visa, CoE, load & work rules are outlined here: [Student visas overview](https://www.curtin.edu.au/study/international-students/living-australia/student-visas/) and [Enrolment & visa info for international students](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/personal-support/international/enrolment-visas/).
*   Offer letters and status updates are managed via **Global Curtin**; typical outcomes may take **8–12 weeks**. [HDR application status (FAQ)](https://www.curtin.edu.au/study/help-support/app/answers/detail/a-id/2652/i-am-an-international-student-applying-for-a-higher-degree-by-research-hdr-course-how-will-i-know-the-status-of-my-application/).

### 3) After You Enrol: Candidature & Progression
#### Duration & load
*   HDR degrees are **time-based** (not semester-based). Typical windows: **PhD: 2–4 EFTSL**; **MRes/MPhil: 1–2 EFTSL**. [Managing your HDR enrolment](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/managing-your-hdr-enrolment/).

#### Supervision & thesis committee
*   You’ll have a **Thesis Chair**, **Principal Supervisor**, and **Co/Associate Supervisor**; establish meeting cadence, record outcomes, and maintain a timeline to meet milestones. [Supervision & tips](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/managing-your-hdr-enrolment/).
*   Supervisor eligibility & responsibilities are set by the Graduate Research School. [Candidacy & supervision (staff portal)](https://staffportal.curtin.edu.au/research/hdr-supervision/candidacy/).

#### Milestones (formal progress reviews)
Curtin uses **three** formal milestones; each requires documentation and an **oral presentation**. [Milestones overview](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/milestones/).
*   **Milestone 1 (Candidacy):** due typically **PhD: by 6 months FT**; **Masters: by 3 months FT**; confirms program definition, facilities, RIG completion, and ethics identification. [Milestone 1 requirements](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/milestones/).
*   **Milestone 2 (Mid‑candidacy):** usually **PhD: by 18 months FT**; **Masters: by 12 months FT**—progress reflection and completion strategy. [Milestone timing (collab/joint HDR page)](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/starting-your-hdr-course/collaborative-joint-hdr-students/).
*   **Milestone 3 (Pre‑submission):** at least **3 months before thesis submission**; final outcomes and contribution. [Milestone timing (collab/joint HDR page)](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/starting-your-hdr-course/collaborative-joint-hdr-students/).

#### Ethics & safety (before data collection)
*   Complete Curtin’s **Research Initiation Guide (RIG)** and relevant **risk assessments**; obtain approvals for **human/animal ethics**, biosafety, radiation/laser, etc., **before** starting data collection. [Ethics & safety](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/ethics-safety/) and detailed **human ethics** steps via **InfoEd**. [Human research ethics](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/ethics-safety/human/).

#### Essential facilities & research support funds
*   Guidelines, reimbursement lists, and forms (including **Essential Facilities**, **Research Support Funds**, **Leave**, etc.) are managed by GRS. [HDR forms & guides](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/hdr-forms/).

### 4) Thesis, Examination & Viva (Oral Defense)
*   **Thesis formats**: traditional, **by compilation** (published papers), or creative work + exegesis; prepare per Curtin’s **Thesis Preparation & Submission Guidelines**. [Thesis & examination](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/prepare-thesis/) and [Thesis submission guides (HDR forms)](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/hdr-forms/).
*   **Viva** (oral examination): **compulsory for doctoral students from 1 Jan 2026**; **compulsory for master’s from 1 Jan 2027** (2025 pilot concluded). [Viva policy & timelines](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/prepare-thesis/).
*   Submission logistics: **Nomination of Examiners**, Turnitin similarity checks, author attributions for compiled papers—see the submission checklist. [Submitting your thesis](https://www.curtin.edu.au/students/essentials/higher-degree-by-research/prepare-thesis/).
*   Library resources for formatting, publishing strategy, and compilation options: [Theses—Research Toolkit](https://researchtoolkit.library.curtin.edu.au/publishing/strategic-publishing/theses/).
